About

Keyun Cheng received the B.Eng. degree in Software Engineering from Sun Yat-Sen University in 2018, the M.Sc. degree in Computer Science from The Chinese University of Hong Kong in 2019, and the Ph.D. degree in Computer Science and Engineering from The Chinese University of Hong Kong in 2025. He was a member of Applied Distributed System Lab (ADSLab) in The Chinese University of Hong Kong, supervised by Prof. Patrick P. C. Lee. His research interests include distributed storage systems and erasure coding. He maintains a reading list.
