About

Keyun Cheng is a Ph.D. student supervised by Patrick P. C. Lee. He is a member of Applied Distributed System Lab (ADSLab). He received the B.Eng degree in Software Engineering from Sun Yat-Sen University in 2018, the M.Sc. degree in Computer Science from The Chinese University of Hong Kong in 2019.

His research interests include distributed storage systems and erasure coding.
