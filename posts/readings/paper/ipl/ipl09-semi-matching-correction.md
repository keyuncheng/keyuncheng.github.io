## Summary

This paper summarizes the approximation algorithm in IPL'06 and corrects the
theoretical bounds for the algorithm. At the end, it also mentioned some
solutions for general scheduling for unrelated parallel machines problem, not
necessarily related to semi-matching for bipartite graphs.
