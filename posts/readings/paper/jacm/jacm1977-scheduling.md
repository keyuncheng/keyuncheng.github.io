## Summary

This paper analyzes the theoretical performances (complexity, worst-case
bounds) for 5 heuristics for the scheduling unrelated machines problem. Then
it provides a heuristic to 2 non-identical processor's scheduling, and a
heuristic to m identical processors.

## Details

* Heuristics to Scheudling Unrelated Machines
    * A: greedily finds the schedule
    * B: sorting by minimum processing time
    * C: sorting by maximum processing time
    * D: Find the most appropriate job among all jobs
    * E: Also traverses the remaining jobs in each round. Very close to D
